# Event validation — the thesis materializing in the real world

The scanner finds *setups*. This file records *outcomes* — real, completed
corporate events in the exact categories the strategy targets — as evidence
that the mechanism is real, not hypothetical. (Collected August 2026.)

## Japan — parent takes a listed subsidiary private

### Toyota Industries (TSE: 6201) — COMPLETED, delisted 1 Jun 2026
The textbook Oyako-Jojo resolution, start to finish:
- **Setup:** a listed member of the Toyota group, long trading cheap while
  holding large cross-holdings of Toyota stock (hidden assets) — the classic
  parent/subsidiary overhang the TSE has been pressuring groups to resolve.
- **Event:** Apr–Jun 2025, the Toyota group (Toyoda family vehicle Toyota
  Fudosan, plus Toyota Motor) launched a ~¥4.7 trillion ($33B) bid to take it
  private. Initial offer ¥16,300/share, later **raised to ¥20,600/share**.
- **Outcome:** tender offer ran Jan–Mar 2026; **63.60% tendered** (above the
  42.01% minimum); settled 30 Mar 2026; **delisted from TSE Prime and Nagoya
  Premier on 1 Jun 2026.**
- **Lesson for the strategy:** the event happens, and the price gets *raised*
  under pressure — but note the initial bid was at a slight discount to the
  pre-announcement price. Early positioning at a deep P/B discount is what turns
  a controversial lowball tender into an acceptable exit; buying *after* the
  run-up is not.

### JX Advanced Metals (TSE: 5016) — parent monetization in progress
- ENEOS listed the subsidiary (¥381B IPO, Mar 2025) "to sharpen focus on ROIC",
  then **trimmed its stake from 42.38% → 35.28%**, booking a ¥86B profit boost.
- A live example of a parent unwinding a listed-subsidiary overhang — the same
  reform pressure, expressed as a sell-down rather than a buy-in.

## India — promoter at / over the 75% ceiling

- SEBI caps promoter holding at 75% (25% minimum public float). In one recent
  quarter **78 listed companies** sat above 75% and were forced to bring
  holdings down — the enforced-compliance side of the same ceiling dynamic.
- **UltraTech Cement:** promoter stake jumped to ~82% after acquiring India
  Cements, above the cap, forcing a ~7% divestment — the mirror image of the
  "creep toward 75%" setup, and proof the ceiling is hard and enforced.

## Market backdrop (real, documented)
- ~40% of Japanese companies traded at P/B < 1 as of Jun 2025; 699 Prime/Standard
  names below 0.5× book; the TSE's "name and shame" reform and its investor note
  on parent-subsidiary listings are ongoing (FSA/JPX, 2025).

*This is qualitative validation of the mechanism, not a backtest. A proper
backtest — enter at signal date, hold to event/stop, measure return, win rate,
drawdown, and capital-tie-up time — still needs a full historical event set fed
through a simulation engine.*
